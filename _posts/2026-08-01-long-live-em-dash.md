I've long been an em-dash user. Probably not always in the right places, but it's been a habit for years — option + shift + dash is a part of my muscle memory when typing. 

But since the rise of AI and the increasing amount of slop making its way onto the internet, the em-dash (as you well know) has become a common tell for when a piece of writing has been outsourced to AI.

As a result, I've found myself deleting em-dashes where I've used them recently. And the thing that's weighing on me is that I want my writing to sound like me, but the way I naturally write now might cause the reader to assume I didn't write it — so I'm second-guessing my own writing habits 🙃

I'm sharing this post as I know others are struggling in various different ways when working with AI, and I want to highlight just one of the many hidden costs of working with more and more with these tools. For the record I am very pro-AI and I think it's making us all more productive when used deliberately — but it's not all upside...

## 🧠 The cost

I spend a big chunk of my day reading and writing. Slack messages, docs, taking meeting notes — so this comes up a lot.

Every time the instinct to em-dash arrives, I stop. Comma? Colon? Semi-colon? Full stop? Or F it and use the em-dash? It's a two-second decision, which is nothing really, right up until it's happened twenty times before lunch and I've spent a chunk of my scarce writing time thinking about punctuation instead of the thing I was actually trying to communicate.

The problem is I think <b>I'm writing worse in order to seem more human</b>. Which in itself is kind of stupid. And maybe nobody actually cares, but it's a cost I've been carrying more and more recently.

Being occasionally misread costs me less than rewriting myself into something safer, and I'd rather spend my attention on <b>what</b> I'm saying than on how it looks like I said it. So, I'm keeping it. For now anyway. Long live the em-dash and all that.

And yes, there are lots of em-dashes in this post. Maybe I just outsourced the whole thing to Claude anyway 😉
